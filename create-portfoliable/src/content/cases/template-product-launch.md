## Context
This template demonstrates a production-grade launch narrative from strategy to post-release stabilization.

## Business Goal
Define a launch motion that grows adoption while preserving quality and support capacity.

## Constraints
- Tight launch window with fixed release date.
- Multi-market messaging requirements.
- Shared engineering and design capacity.

## Approach
1. Align launch tiers (internal, beta, public).
2. Define user-critical flows and success signals.
3. Build instrumentation before growth campaigns.
4. Use progressive rollout with rollback criteria.

## Execution
- Mapped release milestones to ownership matrix.
- Created launch checklist for engineering, design, and content.
- Implemented analytics events for activation and retention.
- Prepared support macros and incident triage playbook.

## Outcomes
- Reduced launch risk through phased rollout.
- Faster incident detection via predefined alerting.
- Clear narrative for portfolio storytelling and technical accountability.

## Learnings
- Instrumentation should be treated as launch-critical scope.
- Decision logs reduce cross-team drift during final sprint.

## Contexto
Este template demonstra uma narrativa de lancamento completa, da estrategia ate a estabilizacao pos-lancamento.

## Objetivo de Negocio
Definir um plano de lancamento que aumente adocao sem comprometer qualidade e capacidade de suporte.

## Restricoes
- Janela curta com data fixa.
- Requisitos de comunicacao para multiplos mercados.
- Capacidade compartilhada entre engenharia e design.

## Abordagem
1. Alinhar etapas do lancamento (interno, beta, publico).
2. Definir fluxos criticos e sinais de sucesso.
3. Instrumentar antes das campanhas de crescimento.
4. Aplicar rollout progressivo com criterio de rollback.

## Execucao
- Mapeamento de marcos e matriz de responsabilidade.
- Checklist de lancamento para engenharia, design e conteudo.
- Eventos de analytics para ativacao e retencao.
- Macros de suporte e playbook de triagem.

## Resultados
- Menor risco com rollout em fases.
- Deteccao mais rapida de incidentes.
- Narrativa clara para storytelling de portfolio.

## Aprendizados
- Instrumentacao deve ser escopo critico de lancamento.
- Registro de decisoes reduz desalinhamento no sprint final.
